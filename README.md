Problem statement
AtliQ hardware is a company which delivers computer hardware & peripheral Manufacturers to his clients, which has several branches throughout India. The sales director of the company is facing a lot of issues in terms of understanding how the business is performing and what are all the problem company is facing currently as the sales are not as expected and declining gradually. And whenever he calls the regional managers to get the current status of the sales and market, as a human behaviour, these people sugar cote the truth and send tons of Excel files instead of disclosing the truth, which made the sales director more frustrated. Humans are not comfortable in consuming numbers from excel files, which is obvious reason for the frustration.

Project Description

Developed an interactive Sales Insights & Performance Dashboard using Power BI to provide actionable insights into sales and profitability. The project analyzes revenue, sales quantity, profit margin, customer performance, product performance, markets, and time-based trends.

Used Power Query for data preparation and transformation and created a structured Power BI data model connecting sales transactions with product, customer, market, and date dimensions. Developed DAX measures for key metrics including Revenue, Sales Quantity, Profit Margin %, Profit, Revenue Contribution %, Revenue LY, Target Difference, and Total Profit Margin.

The dashboard was designed to reduce manual data gathering and provide stakeholders with a centralized view of the latest sales performance. Interactive analysis enables users to compare performance across products, customers, markets, and time periods, helping sales teams identify trends, monitor profitability, and support data-driven decision-making.
